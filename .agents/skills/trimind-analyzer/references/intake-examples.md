# Intake examples

Use when the user's input is vague or missing. Examples are hypothetical.

## What a helpful input contains
- Situation: who, what, where things stand now
- The decision or question
- Constraints: budget, time, regulation, skills
- Desired outcome and deadline
- What is already known and what is not

## Examples of good inputs

**Decision.** "We are a 40-person software company deciding whether to build our own reporting module or buy one. Budget is limited, two senior engineers are available part-time, and customers have asked for it within two quarters. We have no vendor quotes yet."

**Framework.** "I need a scoring model to prioritise 15 modernisation candidates in a regulated environment. Criteria must be defensible in front of an audit committee. I have no data on run costs yet."

**Hypothesis.** "I believe our churn is driven by slow onboarding. We have exit-survey comments but no cohort data. How should I test this before changing the process?"

**Strategic action.** "Our team wants to adopt a new architecture pattern. Leadership is cautious. What is the strongest case, the strongest objection, and a pilot that limits risk?"

## Clarifying questions (pick at most 3)
1. What outcome would make this a success, and by when?
2. Which constraint is truly fixed, and which is negotiable?
3. What data do you already have, and what is a guess?
4. Who must agree for this to happen, and who might resist?
5. How reversible is the decision?
