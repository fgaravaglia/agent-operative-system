# Bias and fallacy catalog for the Examiner

Use as a scan list. Report only the biases that actually plausibly apply, each with its target step.

| # | Bias / fallacy | Test question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirmation bias | Did the reasoning look for evidence against the preferred option, or only for support? |
| 2 | Anchoring | Is an early number or option shaping everything after it without justification? |
| 3 | Sunk cost | Is past spending being treated as a reason to continue? |
| 4 | Survivorship bias | Are we looking only at successes and ignoring those that failed the same way? |
| 5 | Availability heuristic | Is a vivid recent example standing in for the real frequency? |
| 6 | Planning fallacy | Are time and cost estimates based on the best case instead of comparable past projects? |
| 7 | False dichotomy | Are only two options on the table when a third exists? |
| 8 | Base-rate neglect | Is a specific story overriding how often this kind of thing normally happens? |
| 9 | Authority bias | Is a claim accepted because of who said it, not on its evidence? |
| 10 | Correlation as causation | Is a pattern treated as a cause without ruling out a third factor or reverse direction? |
| 11 | Status-quo bias | Is "keep things as they are" being favoured without being evaluated as an option? |
| 12 | Optimism / overconfidence | Are the ranges too narrow? What would a pessimist say about the same numbers? |

## How to report

"E3 → A4: possible planning fallacy. The 6-week estimate matches the best case; comparable efforts in this context usually run longer. Impact: delivery date in the final plan is at risk."
