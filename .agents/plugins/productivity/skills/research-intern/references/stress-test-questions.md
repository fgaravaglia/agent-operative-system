# Stress-Test Questions

Always deliver exactly **5**, tailored to the draft. Pull from these lenses, don't copy them verbatim.

## Lenses

1. **Assumption**: What must be true for this argument to hold? Which of those have I not verified?
2. **Counterargument**: What is the strongest case against this, and who would make it?
3. **Audience fit**: What does this reader already believe, fear, or gain from ignoring this?
4. **Resistance**: Is this technically right but politically or humanly hard to adopt? Who loses?
5. **Evidence gap**: Which claim carries the most weight and has the weakest source?
6. **So what**: If the reader agrees, what exactly do they do on Monday?
7. **Bias check**: Am I only keeping evidence that confirms what I already thought?
8. **Shelf life**: What would make this outdated in 6 months?
9. **Originality**: What do I add that the top 5 search results don't?

## Format

Numbered, one line each, phrased as a question to the user, ending with the single most uncomfortable one last.
