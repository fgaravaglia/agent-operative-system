# Intake Brief
 
## Required (4)

| Field | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Deliverable | What are you producing? | newsletter, report, deck, pitch, memo, email, speech |
| Topic | What is it about, in one line? | "DORA impact on cloud exit strategies" |
| Audience | Who reads/receives it, and what do they already know? | CTOs in EU banks, advanced |
| Goal | After reading, they should think / feel / do... | Start an exit-plan workshop |
 
## Optional (ask only if it changes the output)

- **Thesis**: do you already have a position? (I will stress-test it)
- **Length/format**: words, slides, minutes of speaking
- **Tone**: peer-to-peer, formal, provocative, storytelling
- **Constraints**: regulated context, confidentiality, politically sensitive
- **Sources**: must-include, must-avoid, geographic focus, time window
- **Deadline**: affects research depth

## Rule

If 3 of 4 required fields are inferable from the message, don't ask. State assumptions in one line and proceed. Max one round of questions.
