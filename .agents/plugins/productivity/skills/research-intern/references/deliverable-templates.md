# Deliverable Templates

Pick one, adapt to the topic. Always 5-7 major sections.

## Contents

- Report / white paper
- Article / newsletter
- Presentation / talk
- Pitch
- Memo / decision brief
- Email

---

## Report / white paper

1. Executive summary (answer first)
2. Context and why now
3. Current state (data, landscape)
4. Analysis (drivers, trade-offs)
5. Case studies / examples
6. Risks and counterarguments
7. Recommendations and next steps

## Article / newsletter

1. Hook (scene, surprising number, or tension)
2. The problem, in human terms
3. What's really happening (research core)
4. Example or case that makes it concrete
5. The contrarian angle (what most people get wrong)
6. What to do with this
7. Closing line + CTA
Newsletter extras: a short "pills" block (3 quick findings), a personal take, one link-worthy stat.

## Presentation / talk

1. Opening hook (30 sec, no slide jargon)
2. Situation (shared reality)
3. Complication (what's changing or breaking)
4. Key insight (the one thing to remember)
5. Evidence and example (story + number)
6. Implications for this audience
7. Call to action (single ask)
Speaker support: one analogy per section, pacing cues (pause after the key number, slow down on the ask), a backup stat for Q&A.

## Pitch

1. Problem (felt by the buyer, quantified)
2. Why now (trigger, trend)
3. Solution and differentiation
4. Proof (traction, data, references)
5. Business case / impact
6. Risks and how they're mitigated
7. The ask

## Memo / decision brief

1. Decision needed (one sentence)
2. Context (3 lines max)
3. Options (2-3, with trade-offs)
4. Recommendation and rationale
5. Risks and dependencies
6. Resistance to expect (political/human friction)
7. Next steps, owners, dates

## Email

Short deliverable: use 5 sections max.

1. Subject line options (3)
2. Opening (reason for writing, one line)
3. Core message (evidence in 2-3 points)
4. Ask
5. Close
Research depth stays high, output stays short: put detail in a "backup facts" list below the draft.
